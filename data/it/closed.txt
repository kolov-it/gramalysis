# Italian function words: articles, prepositions (with their contracted
# forms), pronouns, conjunctions. These are answered from this list, not by
# the lexicon service. One reading per line; a form with several readings has
# several lines. A reading of several morphemes joins them with `+`.
#
# form = lemma:Category{feature=value,...} [+ lemma:Category{...}]
#
# Features: gender m/f, number sg/pl, person 1/2/3, definite yes/no,
# case nom (subject) / obl (stressed, after a verb or preposition) /
# acc (direct clitic) / dat (indirect clitic) / refl / gen (ne) / loc (ci, vi),
# clitic yes/no, combining yes (me, te, se, ce, ve, glie before lo/la/li/le/ne).

# --- definite article
# `next` is the sound the following word starts with: c = an ordinary
# consonant (il cane), sc = s + consonant, z, gn, ps, pn, x, y or i + vowel
# (lo zio, lo studente), v = a vowel (l'amico). A form allowed before two kinds
# of sound has a line for each.
il = il:Det{definite=yes,gender=m,number=sg,next=c}
lo = il:Det{definite=yes,gender=m,number=sg,next=sc}
la = il:Det{definite=yes,gender=f,number=sg,next=c}

l' = il:Det{definite=yes,number=sg,next=v}

gli = il:Det{definite=yes,gender=m,number=pl,next=v}
